### About DBHQ

DBHQ is the engineering practice of Daniel Grimes - 26 years building software for organisations where failure was expensive, across UK defence, banking, energy and digital identity. The model is simple: AI does the heavy lifting; a senior engineer decides what to ask, what to build and what ships. The result is delivery in weeks, at production grade - robust, reliable and built to last.

**Need hands, not a hire?** Fixed-price delivery and senior-engineer retainers, remote-first from the UK.
